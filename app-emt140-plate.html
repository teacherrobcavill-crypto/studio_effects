1960s Engineering Guide How Flexural Plate Dispersion, Damper Gap & Dual Piezo Pickups Work ▼ Unfold Guide
A moving-coil driver mounted slightly off-center excites 2D bending waves in the tensioned metal sheet. Because flexural phase velocity increases with frequency ($v_p \propto \sqrt{\omega}$), high-frequency transients reach the two asymmetric piezo contact pickups before low frequencies—creating the unmistakable dense "whip" that made the EMT-140 famous on Abbey Road, Pink Floyd, and Motown vocals.
